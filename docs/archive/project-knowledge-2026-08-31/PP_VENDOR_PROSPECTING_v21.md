### ⚠️ The pricing anomaly — the thing to check first

Homepage advertises **"ANY STRENGTH $89 SEMAGLUTIDE"** and **"ANY STRENGTH $119 TIRZEPATIDE."**

If literal, 30mg semaglutide costs the same as 5mg — which would make them **dramatically the cheapest vendor on PP's GLP-1 price pages**, with a $/mg curve unlike anything else in the dataset.

Structural note for onboarding: their products carry **two variant axes — strength AND vial count (1/2/5/10)**. Vial count is the kit axis (drop all but 1 vial, same as Spartan and Crush). Flat pricing across strengths would still be an outlier worth confirming before it publishes.

Catalog seen on the homepage: Retatrutide ($109–1,590), Semaglutide ($89–699), Tirzepatide ($119–899), BPC-157/TB-500 blend, NAD+, GLOW, KLOW, GHK-Cu.

### What would move it to buildable

1. **COA library check** — real per-batch certificates from a *named* third-party lab, or marketing PDFs?
2. **"Any strength" pricing** — literal, or a headline rate for the smallest vial?
3. **Independent reviews appearing** over the next 60–90 days.

CINC can do (1) and (2) in one pass. (3) is just time.

> **Why wait at all:** the affiliate account costs nothing to hold, and PP's vendor list is only worth what its trustworthiness is worth. A six-week-old vendor with zero external verification is exactly the addition that costs credibility if it goes wrong — and there is no upside to being early.

## 🎯 NEW-VENDOR VETTING STANDARD (new — generalize from the above)

Before a newly-discovered vendor gets a page, check:

1. **Site age** — asset upload dates, copyright year, domain registration. Under ~3 months = wait unless something else is compelling.
2. **Independent review footprint** — Trustpilot, PeptideCritic, PeptiPrices, Reddit. *Absence* isn't disqualifying on its own, but it removes the ability to verify anything.
3. **Physical address + named operating entity.** Email-only contact with no address is on every published red-flag list.
4. **COA library contents**, not just its existence. A `/coas` link proves nothing until a certificate is opened.
5. **Pricing sanity** — dramatically below market on GLP-1s is a known counterfeit signal. Not proof of anything, but it's the check to run rather than skip.

**None of these is individually disqualifying. Three or more together means wait.**

This standard is retrospective as well as forward-looking: it's the check that wasn't formalized when Crush (founded Oct 2025) and Treasure Coast were onboarded — both passed on address, entity, and COA library, so the outcome was fine, but by inspection rather than by rule.
