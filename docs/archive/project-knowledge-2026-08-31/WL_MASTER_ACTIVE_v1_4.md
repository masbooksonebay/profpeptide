## 🤝 Partner Fulfillment Model Research (MAJOR NEW v1.4)

Mark surfaced a sharp operational question this session: **does Capstone hold inventory, or does the partner ship per-order on Capstone's behalf?** Research against the 5 Tier 1 partners reveals the peptide white-label industry mostly operates on **bulk wholesale + white-label** rather than true per-order dropship.

### Three operational paths surfaced

**Path 1 — True dropship (zero inventory):**
- Partner holds inventory, picks/packs/ships per-order in Capstone packaging
- Lowest startup capital, lowest operational complexity
- **YPB only** fits this model — but **big 3 GLPs absent** from YPB catalog (or so research suggests; not yet confirmed)
- Effectively Path A-only

**Path 2 — Bulk wholesale + you hold inventory:**
- Buy in volume tiers (Royal MOQ 100 / Licensed MOQ 20)
- Inventory at your address, cold storage, your fulfillment labor
- Per-order shipping from your inventory
- Higher startup capital (~$5K-$15K per SKU for Royal; smaller for Licensed)

**Path 3 — Bulk source + 3PL fulfillment:**
- Buy bulk from Royal/Licensed/SmartMD
- Inventory sits at RapidFulfillment's CA warehouse (or similar 3PL)
- RF handles order receipt + pick/pack/ship per-order on your behalf
- Two-vendor stack, cleanest separation, adds per-pick fees

### Per-partner fit assessment

| Partner | Manufactures? | White-labels? | Per-order dropship? | Fits stated model? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SmartMD Labs** | ❌ No — sourcing/fulfillment intermediary | Unknown gap | Unknown gap | Unclear; needs discovery |
| **Royal Wholesale** | ✅ Yes — full CDMO | ✅ Yes — explicit | ❌ No — MOQ 100 vials bulk | Partial |
| **YPB** | ✅ Yes (Rocktomic Labs) | ✅ Yes | ✅ **Zero MOQ dropship** | ✅ Best fit, but **big 3 absent** |
| **Licensed Peptides** | ✅ Yes — US GMP-certified | ✅ Yes — 3 labeling options | ❌ Volume tiers (20+ units) | Partial |
| **RapidFulfillment** | ❌ No — pure 3PL | ❌ Not their role | ✅ Yes (as 3PL service) | Different category — needs manufacturer pairing |

### Critical research gap (needs Tier 1 discovery email)

**Question to ask Royal / Licensed / SmartMD:** *Do you offer fulfillment-from-inventory where I prepay for stock and you ship per-order to my customers in Capstone-branded packaging?*

This is the hybrid model that may or may not exist. If yes, Path 3-via-manufacturer (instead of Path 3-via-3PL) becomes viable and simpler. None of the prior research probed this specifically. **This question goes in the 7-question discovery email template.**

### Updated 7-question discovery email template

For Royal Wholesale, Licensed Peptides, SmartMD Labs (and YPB if/when they respond):

1. **Catalog:** Do you carry Sema, Tirz, Reta? (the big 3 question)
2. **MOQ:** What's the minimum order quantity per SKU?
3. **White-label:** Capstone-branded labeling — what tier, what setup process, what file format?
4. **Fulfillment model:** Wholesale ship-to-me only, OR fulfillment-from-inventory where you hold my pre-purchased stock and ship per-order to my customers?
5. **Cold-chain:** Cold-pack standard? Liability if cold chain breaks during transit?
6. **Lead time:** Manufacture → ready-to-ship for restock orders?
7. **Per-order fulfillment cost:** If hybrid model exists, what's the pick-pack-ship fee per unit?

**Question 4 is the gating question** that determines whether the partner can serve Capstone's stated business model.
