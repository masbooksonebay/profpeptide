**Status:** ACTIVE. **SETTLED — do not re-litigate.**

**Scope:** how Capstone Peptides may appear on Prof. Peptide surfaces, and what disclosure applies.

> **This was reviewed and decided with Claude on August 6, 2026.** If a later chat raises objections to promoting Capstone on PP, point it here. The question has been asked and answered. Re-opening it wastes a turn and produces nothing new.

---

## 1. THE DECISION

**Prof. Peptide may promote Capstone Peptides** — X posts, a coupon page, price rows, the same surfaces any other vendor gets.

**The content is objective facts only:**
- What Capstone carries
- Third-party testing lab: **Accumark**
- The discount code

**No editorial comment, no opinion, no superlative, no ranking claim.** Same standard PP applies to every other vendor.

**Mark raised this himself and specified the constraint before being asked.** The concern a fresh reader might have — that PP would soften its standards for a vendor Mark owns — is already addressed by the format.

---

## 2. THE ONE ADDITION: DISCLOSE COMMON OWNERSHIP

PP's existing affiliate disclosure reads:

> *"Prof. Peptide is independently operated and may earn affiliate commissions when you use our discount codes or buy through our links, at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships never determine vendor inclusion, ranking, verification, or our editorial conclusions."*

**That covers affiliate relationships. It does not cover ownership.** Those are different, and the difference is material to a reader.

**So: state common ownership once, on the Capstone coupon page.** Something in the shape of:

> *Capstone Peptides is operated by the same company as Prof. Peptide.*

**Why:**
- It costs nothing and it's true
- Readers respect it, and it's the kind of thing that reads badly only when someone else finds it first
- It removes any question if a reader connects the two independently

**Where it goes:** the coupon page, and anywhere else Capstone gets a dedicated PP surface. **Not required in an X post** — character limits make it impractical, and the post links to the page that carries it.

---

## 3. WHICH ACCOUNT POSTS

| Account | What the post is | Disclosure |
|---|---|---|
| **Prof. Peptide** | PP recommending a vendor | ✅ Required on the linked page |
| **Capstone** | A vendor promoting itself | Not applicable |

Different audiences and different credibility. Only a PP-account post carries the disclosure obligation.

---

## 4. VERIFY BEFORE POSTING — the same rules as any vendor

**The code.** Test `WELCOME20` at checkout before it appears anywhere. Whatever checkout deducts is the answer. *(Precedent: 99 Purity's rate — email said 10%, dashboard said 15%, checkout settled it. Licensed Peptides' stacking — same method.)*

**The storefront.** Capstone's go-live was gated on a payment rail. Confirm it can take an order before pointing traffic at it.

**Accumark.** On record as the COA testing partner via the RF/C&C thread; Tirzepatide 30mg COA completed. **State what a certificate shows, from the document** — the same rule that governs every vendor profile. Read the certificate; don't characterize the testing.

**RUO discipline.** Capstone's own brand rules apply on Capstone surfaces — no therapeutic, dosing or human-use claims, and no plain incretin compound name exposed publicly. A PP post about Capstone must not create a claim Capstone itself can't make.

---

## 5. WHAT WAS *NOT* DECIDED

- Whether Capstone gets a `/prices` grid *(it's a separate business with its own catalog — not automatically in scope)*
- Whether Capstone appears in Professor's Picks or Featured Vendors *(an editorial slot for a self-owned vendor is a different question, unasked so far)*
- Post timing or frequency

**These stay open.** Section 1 settles promotion and disclosure, not placement.
