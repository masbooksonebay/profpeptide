## FORMAT

```
Week [N] Day [N] — [session sentence]

Free 12-week HYROX program 📲 hybridrockstar.app/get

#HYROX #hyroxtraining #hybridtraining #[session tag]
```

**Why this differs from YouTube:**
- **No "link in bio."** TikTok bio URLs aren't clickable, so the phrase promises a tap that doesn't exist. The URL is short enough to read and type — showing it directly is more honest and no less useful.
- **One sentence, not three.** TikTok truncates hard and rewards brevity.
- **No title field** — one caption, hashtags inline.
- **Hashtags carry more weight here** than on YouTube, where they're a modest signal.

**Per upload:** turn on Music copyright check and Content check lite · **clear the auto-filled location** (it defaults to San Clemente — geotagging a national app to one town narrows reach for nothing).

## ⚠️ TWO THINGS FOUND WHILE DRAFTING

**1. W1D2 and W1D5 are the same session** — both 35 min Zone 2, identical stimulus. The captions above differentiate deliberately (D2 describes the effort, D5 frames it as the week's second base run) so the two posts don't read as a duplicate. Same applies on YouTube; the v4 descriptions differ enough already.

**2. 🔴 HR_PROGRAM_EXPORT may be out of sync with the app.**

W1D1 in the export:
```
A1. Back Squat 4×5 @ RPE 6-7
A2. Romanian Deadlift 3×8 @ RPE 6
B1. Walking Lunges 3×20
B2. Plank 3×45 sec
```

The published W1D1 YouTube Short — a screen recording of the app — shows:
```
A1. Back Squat 4×5 @ RPE 7
A2. Romanian Deadlift 3×8 @ RPE 7
B1. Walking Lunges 3×20 with light DB
B2. Single-leg Glute Bridge 3×12 each side
B3. Plank 3×60 sec
```

**Different RPEs, an extra movement, and a longer plank.** HR_SOCIAL already flags the export as an ad-hoc transcription with no generator, not in the repo — and every carousel and reel is built from it.

**The app is the artifact; the export is a document describing it.** When they disagree, the app wins. Worth reconciling before more sessions are drafted from the export — a wrong set/rep scheme on a published carousel is a correctness problem, not a cosmetic one.

The Week 1 captions above are written at a level that's true either way (movement names, not numbers). Later weeks' YouTube descriptions **do** cite specific sets and reps, so those are the ones exposed if the export has drifted.
