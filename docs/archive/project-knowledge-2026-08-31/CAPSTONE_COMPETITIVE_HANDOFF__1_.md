## 1. 🎯 THE AGGREGATORS ARE THE ACQUISITION CHANNEL — AND THEY'RE JOINABLE

Two aggregators own the comparison/review layer of this market. There are only two of consequence; the space is uncrowded.

**What this means for Capstone:**
- **An affiliate program is table stakes.** All 57 PeptiPrices vendors run a code. No code → not listed → invisible to the comparison layer.
- **PeptideCritic has a formal "Vendor Program" intake page** (footer). Vendors apply to them.
- **"★Premium Partner" = PAID PLACEMENT.** Six vendors carry it (Ion, Genpeptide, EZ, Glacier, Orbitrex, Bioedge). Notably both of the smallest — Genpeptide 56K, Bioedge 32K — carry it. **The badge tracks who pays, not who's big.** So placement is purchasable regardless of size. That's a launch lever.
- **GoAffPro is the dominant affiliate platform** — 62% of Orbitrex's referrals, 20% of SwissChems', 13% of EZ's. If Capstone runs its program on GoAffPro, it's discoverable by every affiliate mining that directory (which is how PP found 111 vendors).
- PeptideCritic is the one to prioritize: **growing +31% with a real organic footprint**; PeptiPrices is **dying (−54%, 9.5% organic, undefended)**.

## 5. ✅ WHAT AGGREGATORS REWARD (i.e. what to build in from day one)

PeptideCritic's directory displays these signals per vendor — they're effectively the scorecard:
- **"Verified" badge** and **"Batch #s"** — published per-batch COAs. This is the trust signal both aggregators surface.
- **Star rating + review count** — user-generated, free account required. Capstone will start at zero; early reviews matter disproportionately.
- **Est. year** — new vendors are visibly new (most of the 2025–2026 cohort has thin reviews).
- **Order volume tier** (PeptiPrices shows "50K+ orders", "250K+ orders").
- **Price/mg** — the primary sort on both sites. This is the axis you compete on.
- **Shipping speed** (PeptiPrices shows "1–3 days" etc.) and **payment methods** (crypto ₿ icons are common and displayed).

⚠️ **Ratings are unreliable — do not benchmark against them.** The two platforms flatly contradict each other: Modern Aminos is 4.9 (1,569 reviews) on PeptiPrices and 3.6 (10) on PeptideCritic. Atomik Labz is 2.9 vs 4.5. Small samples, self-hosted, and both platforms have affiliate relationships with the vendors they rate. Use them as weak signals only.

## 6. 🏷️ THE GLP CODE-NAME CONVENTION IS INDUSTRY-WIDE

Every serious vendor obfuscates incretin compound names publicly:
- PeptiPrices, PeptideCritic, Amino Club, Aero all use variants: **GLP-3R / GLP3-RT / GLP-3 (RT)** = Retatrutide · **GLP-2T / GLP1-Tirz** = Tirzepatide · **GLP-1S / GLP1-Semaglutide** = Semaglutide
- Capstone's vial photos already show **GLP-1S / GLP-2T / GLP-3R** — consistent with the convention. Good.
- Aggregators expect this and handle the mapping on their end.

## 7. ⚠️ CONFLICT OF INTEREST — FLAG FOR MARK

Mark owns **both** Prof. Peptide (an independent comparison/review site) and Capstone Peptides (a vendor). PP's coupon page currently states: *"Affiliate relationships never determine vendor inclusion, ranking, verification, or our editorial conclusions."* PP's entire brand equity is independence.

**Listing Capstone on PP — or ranking it favorably — without prominent disclosure of common ownership would be the exact credibility failure PP has been built to avoid.** It's also precisely the criticism PeptideCritic draws for running its own store alongside its reviews.

This isn't a reason not to do it — it's a reason to decide the disclosure posture *deliberately and in advance*, not discover it later. Options: exclude Capstone from PP entirely; include with explicit "owned by the same company" labeling; or keep the brands publicly separate and accept that the link is discoverable (SCS LLC / Capstone Holdings LLC are separate entities, but common ownership is traceable).
