## 📐 PER-SURFACE FORMAT — they are not interchangeable

The same MP4 goes everywhere; **the metadata does not port.**

### YouTube Shorts
Title carries the search phrase + week/day + three hashtags. Description is three sentences plus a **live App Store link**. Full set of 64 in **HR_YOUTUBE_TITLES_DESCRIPTIONS v4**.

```
HYROX Zone 2 Easy Run | Week 1 Day 5 #HYROX #hyroxtraining #hybridtraining
```

**The description link is YouTube's structural edge** — it's the only surface besides the website where a clickable link sits with the content.

### TikTok
**Different in four ways, and the YouTube format does not transfer:**
1. **No clickable caption link** — `hybridrockstar.app/get` lives in the bio; the caption says "link in bio"
2. **Short captions** — TikTok truncates hard; the three-sentence YouTube form is far too long
3. **No title field** — one caption, hashtags inline
4. **Hashtags carry more weight** than on YouTube, where they're a modest signal

Working format:

```
Week 1 Day 1 — full lower-body strength. Back squat, RDL, and
calibration to find your working weights for the cycle.

Free 12-week HYROX program 🔗 link in bio

#HYROX #hyroxtraining #hybridtraining #strengthtraining
```

**Upload settings:** turn on **Music copyright check** and **Content check lite** — both are pre-publish diagnostics, not restrictions, and the reels carry a soundtrack. **Clear the auto-filled location** (it defaulted to San Clemente) — geotagging a national app's content to one town narrows reach for no benefit.

### Instagram · Facebook · Threads
Per HR_SOCIAL v13. Facebook and Threads inherit whatever Instagram posts.

### X
Occasional and undefined. **Note:** PP has a mature X practice (4-post anatomy, research standard, card mechanics) that HR does not use. If X becomes a real HR channel, that's the model to adapt — not to reinvent.

## 🏠 hybridrockstar.app — THE ONLY SURFACE YOU OWN

Six of the seven surfaces are **rented**. Reach comes from Meta, Google, and ByteDance, and each keeps the audience. The website is the exception.

**What makes it different:**
- The App Store link sits with the content — no "link in bio," no description tap
- Content is **indexed and searchable**, and it accrues rather than scrolling away
- Sixty workout videos on an owned domain is a genuine SEO asset

**The precedent is already in the portfolio.** PP's entire strategy rests on owning searchable pages, and it works — the coupon pages produce revenue through search results. HR currently spends its content on platforms that keep the audience and gives its own domain a few posts.

**Currently:** a few sessions posted. **Not scoped.** Open questions before it becomes a workstream — what a session page looks like, whether it's all 64 or a subset, whether the reel embeds or the carousel does the work, and how it links back to the App Store.

Mark has flagged this as something he plans to develop. **Of everything in this doc, it's the surface with the most durable upside.**

## 🎯 WHAT ACTUALLY MOVES THE NUMBERS

**Distribution is not the constraint. Retention is.**

YouTube views ran **96 → 45 → 34 → 5** across the first four Shorts. That is not a reach problem that more surfaces solve — the same asset landing on seven platforms lands the same way on all seven.

Every source consulted on YouTube ranking put **watch time, retention, and click-through far above hashtags**. The equivalent on TikTok is completion rate. Both come down to whether the first second stops the scroll.

**One observable variable:** W1D1 is the only Short that performed, and it's the only one whose opening frame reads **"FULL 12-WEEK HYROX PROGRAM"** rather than the session name. One data point, not a finding — but it's the obvious thing to test, it's a single change to the CD reel hook, and it applies to all seven surfaces at once.

**Test that before adding an eighth platform.**

## 📋 OPERATING NOTES

**Batch, don't drip.** YouTube Studio and TikTok Studio both schedule at upload. Seven in one sitting with staggered publish dates gives a daily cadence without a daily task.

**Test before committing.** TikTok is unproven for HR. **Post five to seven, then compare against YouTube.** If TikTok returns hundreds where YouTube returns single digits, it becomes the priority surface and the effort reallocates. If it also returns single digits, that's learned cheaply.

**Review per-surface performance after two weeks** and decide where the daily minute goes. It probably isn't "all three equally."

**Automation ceiling:** CC has no browser. CINC has a browser but **cannot operate a local file picker**, so it cannot select an MP4. Manual uploads are manual regardless of authorization. The only automation available is what the platforms provide — Meta's cross-posting, and the schedulers.
