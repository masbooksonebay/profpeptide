## HOW TO USE

**Titles** follow the locked convention: `HYROX [search phrase] | Week N Day N | Hybrid Rockstar`. The middle slot is a **search phrase, never the app's session name**.

**Descriptions** — the fixed parts go in **YouTube Studio → Settings → Upload defaults** once. Then per video you only paste the title and swap the middle sentence and the final hashtag.

Fixed block for upload defaults:

```
Week [N] Day [N] of the free 12-week HYROX training program in the
Hybrid Rockstar app. [SESSION SENTENCE]. Structured workouts, race
prep, and AI coaching that scales to any level.

📲 Free on iOS: hybridrockstar.app/get

#Shorts #HYROX #hyroxtraining #hybridtraining #[TAG]
```

> **Note on the opening clause.** W1D1 shipped as "Day 1 of the free 12-week…", which reads correctly only because Day 1 was also the program's first day. Everything from here uses **"Week N Day N of the free 12-week…"** so it stays truthful. Don't retro-edit W1D1.

**Filenames:** export each MP4 as the full title string. YouTube pre-fills the title from the filename, which removes one paste per video.

**Session tags used:** `#strengthtraining` · `#running` · `#hyroxstations` · `#racesimulation` · `#raceday`
