## 🆕 BLENDS / STACKS — discovery RESOLVED (key finding this session)

**Context:** 9 new blend/combo peptide pages + 2 stack pages built in the last few days, under `/peptides/[slug]`:
`cjc-1295-dac-ipamorelin`, `kpv-bpc-157`, `mk-677-ipamorelin`, `nad-mots-c-5-amino-1mq`, `pt-141-oxytocin`, `semaglutide-bpc-157`, `sermorelin-ipamorelin`, `tesamorelin-ipamorelin`, `tirzepatide-bpc-157`, plus `gh-stack`, `wolverine-stack`. (11 total.)

**Investigation path + finding (LOCKED — don't re-litigate):**
1. Local `public/sitemap-0.xml` (182 URLs, dated May 29) was MISSING 4 of the 11 (mk-677-ipamorelin, nad-mots-c-5-amino-1mq, pt-141-oxytocin, tesamorelin-ipamorelin). Initial hypothesis: stale/incomplete sitemap needs regen+commit+push.
2. **Hypothesis was WRONG.** Verified the LIVE sitemap directly: `curl https://profpeptide.com/sitemap-0.xml` = **186 URLs**, and **all 11 blends present** (incl. the 4 missing locally).
3. **Root mechanism:** sitemap is generated by **`next-sitemap` (v4.2.3)** via the build script `next build && next-sitemap`. Vercel regenerates the sitemap on EVERY deploy from current routes. So the live sitemap is always current; the local `public/sitemap-0.xml` is just a **stale generated artifact**.

**Locked conclusions:**
- ✅ **All 11 blends are in the live sitemap → already discoverable by Google. No sitemap fix needed.** No regen, no commit, no push.
- ✅ The "parked" local `public/sitemap-0.xml` being stale is **harmless** — it's regenerated on every Vercel build. **Do NOT hand-commit it** (it's a build artifact; committing a stale local copy is pointless and risks confusion). This retroactively explains why parking it all along was correct.
- ✅ **Why the blends aren't indexed yet:** they're simply NEW pages in Google's crawl queue, and GSC's coverage data is stale to May 24 (predates them). New-but-sitemapped pages index on their own over days–weeks. No discovery gap.

**To ACCELERATE (optional, not required):** queue the 2 highest-value blends as next session's priority 5-URL batch — **`/peptides/semaglutide-bpc-157`** + **`/peptides/tirzepatide-bpc-157`** (GLP blends = highest search volume). The rest come in via the sitemap.
